## Objective

Verify the assembled MR.2 shared Admin refinement foundation across materially
relevant Admin surfaces, confirm that shared primitives remain single-source and
exception-aware, preserve accepted representative evidence, reconcile material
current-state documentation and repository planning at the applicable closure
points, and close MR.2 without expanding into domain-specific refinement.

WU8 is a verification and closure unit. It does not introduce new product,
domain, workflow, lifecycle, ownership, schema, persistence, or visual-design
capability.

## Preconditions and accepted evidence

WU8 must not claim closure readiness unless:

- WU5 shared-primitive scope and exception model are accepted;
- WU6 shared-artifact implementation is accepted;
- WU7 representative adoption/propagation proof is accepted;
- no unresolved shared-foundation defect materially undermines the assembled
  result; and
- remaining domain-specific refinement has an explicit owner or disposition
  outside MR.2.

The accepted WU7 representative proof set is reusable closure evidence:

- **Core Webcore Content Admin:** list plus create/edit forms;
- **System Manager:** Modules/reference surface; and
- **Media Manager:** library/list plus upload surfaces.

WU8 does not require re-migration or broad re-testing of these surfaces without
a concrete regression or material conflict signal. Source-level consistency
review may cover remaining Admin consumers. Users & Access, Settings, Taxonomy,
Navigation, Theme, Form Manager, and Dashboard are not WU8 migration or
refinement targets; accepted evidence may be reused where no material conflict
signal exists.

## Cross-surface verification scope

WU8 must verify, where materially applicable:

- canonical shared primitive single-source behavior;
- page-heading consistency, with no standard eyebrow requirement and any
  remaining local kicker or eyebrow-like treatment classified as a bounded
  local exception only where justified;
- surface/card treatment consistency;
- toolbar/search/filter treatment;
- form/helper treatment;
- button/action placement and sizing;
- modal/dialog action rhythm;
- typography and spacing consistency;
- already accepted selector-level radius/flat-surface dispositions;
- responsive/mobile behavior;
- accessibility behavior;
- explicit semantic exceptions;
- System Manager shared presentation consistency without taking domain ownership;
- no accidental restoration of retired product-facing managers; and
- truthful empty/unsupported states rather than fabricated capability.

WU8 authorizes no global flattening, no global rounding, and no new visual
policy. Legitimate selector- and domain-specific surface exceptions remain
explicit and are not normalized by visual similarity alone.

## Ownership and architecture checks

WU8 must confirm that MR.2 did not:

- transfer domain behavior into shared presentation artifacts;
- recreate standalone Module Manager ownership;
- recreate standalone Settings Manager product ownership;
- restore Redirect Manager;
- silently decide Taxonomy Manager final disposition without sufficient evidence;
- make Dashboard composition part of MR.2;
- pull future per-Bundled-Module refinement back into MR.2; or
- introduce placeholder Security, Email, or System capability where authoritative
  capability does not exist.

Consumers retain route, workflow, data, lifecycle, action, permission,
authorization, validation, and domain-specific interaction semantics. Shared
artifacts own presentation semantics only.

System, Site, Security, Email, System Health, and other parent-surface concerns
must either remain consistent with their accepted owner or be carried forward
with a truthful bounded disposition.

## Closure acceptance criteria

MR.2 may close only when evidence demonstrates that:

1. intended shared primitives are single-source and propagate correctly;
2. accepted selector-level and domain-specific exceptions are explicit and
   bounded, without global flattening or rounding;
3. page-heading consistency is preserved without a standard eyebrow requirement;
4. accepted WU7 representative evidence remains valid, with no re-migration or
   broad retest absent a concrete regression signal;
5. representative and cross-surface behavior remains responsive and accessible;
6. domain semantics and ownership remain unchanged unless separately authorized;
7. no retired-manager ownership was accidentally restored;
8. no hidden per-module refinement was smuggled into MR.2;
9. the Shared File Intake planning payload is preserved with its resolved
   GPT/user disposition as a future cross-surface concept outside MR.2 and not
   implementation-authorized;
10. materially relevant repository documentation is no longer misleading;
11. old Media WU5 forward-scope ambiguity is durably reconciled without
    rewriting its historical authority; and
12. remaining domain refinement has explicit future ownership rather than being
    silently dropped.

## Verification direction

Use the smallest reliable verification chain:

- source-level shared primitive consistency and ownership review;
- focused representative rendering/presentation evidence, reusing accepted
  WU7 results unless a concrete regression or conflict signal exists;
- targeted responsive/mobile and accessibility checks where materially affected;
- focused review of remaining consumers without treating them as migration
  targets;
- focused current-state documentation consistency audit;
- old Media WU5 provenance and forward-scope audit;
- branch-lifecycle audit; and
- closure-time repository Workplan reconciliation.

Broad historical regression repetition is not required without a concrete
impact or regression signal. WU8 must not repair the accepted historical Media
validation debt as part of closure.

## Closure result and authorization boundary

WU8 closure establishes completion of the MR.2 shared Admin refinement scope.
It does not close future Media, Navigation, Theme, Users & Access, Form
Manager, Dashboard, or other dedicated workstreams.

Routine bounded WU8 execution may proceed through a GPT-framed Agent Instruction
under the accepted and promoted MR.2 workstream authorization. Promotion of
this contract does not itself execute WU8 verification or close MR.2.

Fresh explicit user approval remains required only when execution crosses a real
governance boundary, including scope expansion outside accepted/promoted MR.2
scope, Deferred Item adoption, an unresolved product or architecture decision,
destructive or irreversible action, production reconciliation or similarly
sensitive operation, release/tag/publication/external distribution, or another
approval gate explicitly reserved to the user.
